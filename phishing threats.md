# Common Cybersecurity Threats

## 1. Phishing
Phishing is a cyberattack where criminals use fraudulent emails, messages or websites to trick people into revealing sensitive information.

**Prevention:**
- Verify email senders.
- Avoid suspicious links.
- Never share passwords.

## 2. Malware
Malware is malicious software designed to damage systems, steal information or disrupt computer operations.

**Prevention:**
- Install trusted antivirus software.
- Keep software updated.
- Avoid downloading unknown files.

## 3. Social Engineering
Social engineering involves manipulating people into revealing confidential information or performing unsafe actions.

**Prevention:**
- Verify unusual requests.
- Do not share confidential information.
- Be cautious of unexpected phone calls and messages.
